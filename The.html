The Light of Tabor
A Hesychast's Vigil · Ἡ Μεταμόρφωσις
On the holy mountain where Christ shone with uncreated light,
you keep the night vigil alone.

The logismoi — the eight passions of the fallen world —
arise from darkness to extinguish the prayer of your heart.

Maintain the Jesus Prayer. Let the divine light repel the darkness.
Hold the vigil until dawn breaks over Tabor.
